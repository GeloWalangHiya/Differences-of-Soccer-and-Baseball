What are soccer and baseball?

Both are team sports played outdoors with a ball, but the ball is used in very different ways.

Soccer

Kick the ball into the goal

Two teams of eleven players run around a large rectangular field. The aim is to move the ball with your feet, body and head, and put it into the other team's goal.

  • Only the goalkeeper may use their hands, and only near their own goal.
  • A match has two halves of 45 minutes.
  • The team with more goals at the end wins.
Baseball

Hit the ball and run the bases

Two teams of nine players take turns. One team pitches and fields, and the other bats. A batter hits the ball and runs around four bases to get back to home plate.

  • The pitcher throws, the batter swings with a bat, and fielders wear gloves.
  • A game has nine innings, and there is no clock.
  • Each run is one point, and the team with more runs wins.

    How to do it, step by step

    Try the basic move from each sport.

    Soccer: pass the ball

    Five moves, in this order.

    1. Look at your targetPick the teammate or spot you want to reach, then step toward the ball.
    2. Plant your standing footPlace it beside the ball, pointing at the target.
    3. Swing your kicking leg backKeep your ankle firm and your eyes on the ball.
    4. Strike with the inside of your footHit the middle of the ball. This is the most accurate part of the foot.
    5. Follow throughLet your leg swing toward the target as the ball leaves.

    Baseball: throw the ball

    Five moves, in this order.

    1. Grip the ballPut your first two fingers and thumb across the seams.
    2. Turn sidewaysPoint your glove-side shoulder at the target.
    3. Step toward the targetTake a stride with your front foot while your throwing arm goes back.
    4. Bring your arm up and overKeep your elbow high as your arm moves forward.
    5. Release and follow throughLet go of the ball in front of you, and let your arm finish across your body.
